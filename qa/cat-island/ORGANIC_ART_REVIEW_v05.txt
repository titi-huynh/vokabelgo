Art assessment / remaining limits
Softer fruit silhouettes and less repetitive arrangement improve the closeup.
This remains stylized 3D, below the hand-painted reference's artistic finish.
Dragonfruit bracts, cat expression, weathering and painted material variation
still need authored art refinement. This pass does not claim final art approval.
The boat is a static mesh with scene-level buoyancy, not a rigged character.
The new asset is larger than v04; device performance has not been benchmarked.
