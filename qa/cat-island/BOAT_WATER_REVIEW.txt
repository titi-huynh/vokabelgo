Water implementation
- Three analytic travelling waves, with the same height field used by buoyancy.
- Boat pitch and roll sampled across hull length and width.
- Planar reflected boat rendered to a local target; below-water hull clipped out.
- Thin irregular waterline contact detail and gently travelling local ripples.
- World-space foam and two widening wake ribbons that persist behind the boat
  and fade independently after it moves away.
- Surface patch follows the vessel while wave sampling stays in world coordinates.
- Reflection resolution, water grid and pixel ratio reduced on mobile.
- Pause freezes simulation and stops redundant rendering after camera settling.
- Hidden documents stop simulation; reduced-motion preference starts paused.
