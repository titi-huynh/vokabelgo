Cat Goes Fishing describes starting with basic equipment and distinct fish behaviors. Cornerpond describes relaxed automatic fishing with occasional attention for big catches. Fischer describes a quiet study/work companion. Steam screenshots/image-search results also surveyed for simple silhouettes and clear separation of water and fishing action. This was a visual/material survey, not hands-on play or a verified frame-by-frame study of their animation.

Application to our prototype (our design choices, not claims about exact game animation):
- Quiet rests occupy most time, rare events earn attention.
- Clear rod/line/float connection and reaction beats make fishing legible.
- Anticipation -> surprise -> effort -> release -> smile -> settle for a catch.
- Dragonfly approaches, gaze changes first, then head follows with delay; hold before returning.
- Independent breathing, head and tail, no uniform pulsing of the whole character.
- No copied game assets, characters, music, animation tracks or logos.
