1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & TARGET ART DIRECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is a Blender art-direction prototype for VokabelGo's Vietnamese-inspired
floating cat market. It improves the v01 boat shapes, character readability,
color separation and counter props. Plants, canopy, basket weaving and water
remain simplified. This is not finished commercial game art or a tested web asset.

Key Artistic Pillars:
- Vietnamese-inspired River Motifs: Stylized wooden market boats with
  traditional painted protective eyes ("Mắt ghe"), tall curved bamboo display pole
  ("Cây bẹo") with hanging produce, woven bamboo baskets ("Thúng tre / Mẹt tre"),
  simplified conical-hat-inspired accessories, and tropical fruits (dragonfruit, mangosteen, bananas,
  watermelon, citrus).
- Lovable Chibi Feline Proportions: Hero cat Mochi features chubby cheeks, sculpted
  ears with pink inner cavities, expressive glossy eyes with dual catchlights, a
  tailored jade apron with recipe tag, and an active serving pose.
- Cohesive Cozy Game Atmosphere: Warm golden-hour sun, soft sky fill, and gentle
  river bounce lighting with tactile material separation across weathered teak wood,
  glazed ceramics, woven bamboo, and matte canvas.
- Proposed Isometric Composition: Tightened camera framing (orthographic scale 10.6)
  that centers on the selling boat and customer interaction while preserving
  navigation lanes and deliberate space for future vocabulary/order cards.


2. OFFICIAL RESEARCH SOURCES & OBSERVED PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The following official pages were checked during review. Artistic principles
below are our interpretation, not statements by their developers or evidence
that the games were played. Original user images remain the main visual brief.

1. Ooblets (Glumberland)
   - Official Media & Presskit: https://ooblets.com/media/
   - Borrowed Principles: Chunky, tactile low-poly geometry with soft bevels;
     expressive, lovable creature proportions (large heads, chubby lower cheeks,
     distinct eye catchlights); warm pastel-to-saturated color harmony; playful
     chunky accessories.

2. Garden Galaxy (Anneka Tran)
   - Official Steam Store & Media: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1970460/Garden_Galaxy/
   - Borrowed Principles: Micro-diorama cluster presentation; tactile separation
     between materials (woven reed, clay, wood, paper); narrative props clustered
     around gameplay/story focal points rather than dispersed across an empty field.

3. Alba: A Wildlife Adventure (ustwo games)
   - Official Media Page: https://www.albawildlife.com
   - Borrowed Principles: Warm coastal/island sunlight with crisp yet soft directional
     shadows; vibrant Mediterranean/tropical flora clusters; hand-crafted wooden
     piers with natural plank variations and heavy dock pilings.

4. Spiritfarer (Thunder Lotus Games)
   - Official Game Page and Gallery: https://thunderlotusgames.com/games/spiritfarer/
   - Borrowed Principles: Floating community life; multi-tiered boat living with
     graceful cloth awnings that provide shelter without obscuring character faces;
     authentic marine lifestyle props (lanterns, teapots, wrapped cargo, paddles).

Additional preliminary direction: Animal Crossing: New Horizons was suggested
as a visual reference. A specific Nintendo boat-art source was not verified in
this review, so no detailed source attribution is made here.


3. HONEST CRITIQUE OF v01 WEAKNESSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Generic & Vacant Composition: A giant flat 15x12m turquoise water slab occupied
  a large part of the frame as empty space, making the main boat feel small,
  marooned, and lost in an unlit void.
- Primitive Cat Models: Cats were rigid stacks of primitive spheres with flat bead
  eyes, cone ears lacking cavities, sunken bodies behind tall benches, and zero active
  paws or limb posing.
- Face-Obscuring Flat Hats: Oversized flat disc-like hats completely shadowed and
  obscured the cat faces and eyes from the isometric camera angle.
- Unconvincing Boat Geometry: Hulls were straight low-polygon prisms with wire-like
  gunwales floating disconnected in mid-air on support rings; no internal ribbing,
  no sheer curve, and no Vietnamese cultural identifiers.
- Sparse & Repetitive Props: Empty wooden crates, generic yellow spheres for fruits,
  a bare stick with a ball for the "cây bẹo", and repetitive faceted icosphere blobs
  for island trees.
- Flat, Washed-Out Lighting: Ambient-heavy lighting with no golden warmth, no rim
  separation, and pale materials that made the scene look like cheap pastel plastic.


4. CONCRETE BEFORE / AFTER CHANGES (v01 vs v02)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Main Flagship Boat ("Ghe Bán Hàng Nam Bộ"):
   - v01: Boxy generic prism with hovering wire rail and straight deck boards.
   - v02: Custom 7-station lofted Ghe Tam Bản hull with upward flared sheer,
     beveled teak gunwales, structural internal rib frames (be xuồng), alternating
     honey/pale wood floorboards, woven rope bumpers (phao chống va), and iconic
     decorative boat-eye motifs ("Mắt ghe") on the bow. Cultural meaning and
     historical construction accuracy have not been independently audited.

B. Main Shopkeeper Cat (Mochi):
   - v01: Sunken spherical torso, tiny bead eyes, flat disc hat hiding the face.
   - v02: Scaled up to hero proportions (+18%); large rounded head with chubby
     cheeks; sculpted ears with pink inner cavities and tufts; glossy dark eyes
     with dual sparkling catchlights; cream coat with marmalade ginger tabby patch;
     warm coral neckerchief; tailored jade apron with recipe tag; active serving
     pose holding a fresh dragonfruit towards approaching customers.

C. Cây Bẹo Display Pole:
   - v01: A bare stick with a single yellow ball.
   - v02: A tall 3.3m curved bamboo mast with distinct nodes mounted firmly at
     the bow, suspending realistic produce samples (pineapple with spiky crown,
     banana bunch, and watermelon) with natural jute twine.

D. Sales Counter & Merchandise:
   - v01: 3 tiny empty/generic wooden crates on a high flat board.
   - v02: Open, hollow woven bamboo baskets (Thúng Tre) with thick rims and cane
     bands, overflowing with distinct tropical fruits:
     * Basket 1: Vivid magenta dragonfruits (Thanh long) with curling green tips.
     * Basket 2: Deep purple royal mangosteens (Măng cụt) with green calyxes.
     * Basket 3: Plump golden citrus/oranges with leaf stems.
     * Awning Ridge: Hanging ripe bunch of 8 golden bananas.
     * Rear Cargo: Wooden crate with whole striped watermelon and cut red slice.
     * Narrative Props: Open guest recipe book on slanted stand, lotus-leaf
       takeout parcels tied with cross-twine, countertop chalkboard price sign,
       and a traditional glazed Vietnamese teapot with cups on a bamboo tray.

E. Customer Boats & Characters:
   - v01: 3 identical tiny boats with identical sitting cats and colliding oars.
   - v02: 3 completely unique visitor boats and cats:
     * Cinnamon (Left Sampan): Ginger tabby with Nón Lá tilted back 45° so face
       and ears are 100% visible; active reaching pose offering a brass coin;
       cargo of fresh lotus blossoms; paddle stowed cleanly inside the hull.
     * Pepper (Right Canoe): Cool blue-gray cat wearing a woven straw boater hat;
       cargo of stacked glazed ceramic water jars (chum sành); paddle stowed.
     * Lily (Background Sampan): Calico cat relaxing with a pink ear blossom,
       enjoying fresh young coconut water with a bamboo straw.

F. Island & Learning Pavilion:
   - v01: Flat sand cylinder with 3 identical icosphere tree blobs.
   - v02: Multi-tiered riverbank island with warm sand, grassy plateau, and river
     stones; stilt reading pavilion (Vọng Lâu) with terracotta hip roof, curved
     brackets, and glowing paper lantern; low study table with open Vietnamese
     vocabulary scroll, inkstone, and calligraphy brush; authentic banana palm
     (Cây chuối) with arching broad leaves and purple flower bud; daisy patches.

G. Composition & Lighting:
   - v01: Wide orthographic scale (17.7), leaving huge empty water slab.
   - v02: Focused orthographic scale (10.6 for Overview, 5.4 for Close-up) centering
     on the main boat and customer circle; warm 4.4-strength golden sun, soft sky
     fill, and river water bounce lighting using Blender's modern AgX color management.

6. KNOWN LIMITATIONS & FUTURE WEB GAME EXPORT ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Real-Time Web Optimization:
  The current scene is authored for offline Cycles beauty rendering and art direction
  evaluation (55,175 evaluated triangles; no browser performance measurement).
  For future export to WebGL/Three.js/Babylon.js:
  1. Bake high-frequency bevel normals and ambient occlusion into compact atlases.
  2. Collapse modifier stacks and reduce circular curve bevel resolutions (e.g.,
     paddle shafts and gunwales from 24/18 sides to 8/12 sides).
  3. Combine static boat assemblies into single unified meshes with shared materials
     to reduce draw calls from ~500 to under 25.
- Shadow & Ripple Performance:
  Real-time web shaders should replace procedural Cycles glass/water transmission
  with a simple stylized planar water shader using scrolling foam/wake textures.
- Non-Destructive Modularity:
  All boat assemblies (Main Market Flagship, Cinnamon, Pepper, Lily, Island) are
  parented to individual root empties with clear transforms, allowing gameplay
  programmers to reposition, animate, or dock them independently in future stages.
